09 / Questions

Asked a lot.

Still stuck? Ask us on WhatsApp

How does ordering work?

Add your pieces to the bag, fill in your name, phone and address, and tap Order now. Your order is placed straight away and you get an order ID — use it on the tracking page any time.

Do you have cash on delivery?

Yes, COD is available across most of India, with a ₹99 cash handling charge added to the order. Pay by UPI or GPay when you order and there's no extra charge.

How long does delivery take?

We dispatch within 48 hours of confirming. Most cities receive it in 3–6 days; you can follow it any time on the tracking page with your order ID.

What if the size doesn't fit?

Message us within 7 days of delivery. If the piece is unworn with tags on, we'll exchange the size.

Are the colours true to the photos?

Photos are of our actual stock, retouched onto a clean white background, so shade can differ slightly from what you see. Washed pieces also vary a little piece to piece. Ask on WhatsApp and we'll send a real photo before you order.

How do I hear about new drops first?

Join the 52 Alive WhatsApp community. Drops, restocks and members-only prices go there before anywhere else.
