09 / Questions

Asked a lot.

Still stuck? Ask us on WhatsApp

How does ordering on WhatsApp work?

Fill the order form and tap Order on WhatsApp. Your full order — items, sizes, colours and address — opens in a WhatsApp chat with us. Send it, and we reply to confirm stock and the final total.

Do you have cash on delivery?

Yes, COD is available across most of India. You can also pay by UPI once we confirm your order.

How long does delivery take?

We dispatch within 48 hours of confirming. Most cities receive it in 3–6 days; we share tracking on WhatsApp.

What if the size doesn't fit?

Message us within 7 days of delivery. If the piece is unworn with tags on, we'll exchange the size.

Are the colours true to the photos?

All photos are of the actual stock, shot in daylight. Washed pieces vary slightly piece to piece — that's the wash.

How do I hear about new drops first?

Join the 52 Alive WhatsApp community. Drops, restocks and members-only prices go there before anywhere else.
